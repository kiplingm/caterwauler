Help

Getting started
Add songs in the Songbook tab, give each one a status, and set your vocal range in Settings. Everything else — Sing Now, fit coloring, Recommendations — builds on those three things.
Sing Now vs. Songbook
Sing Now is the home screen — a short, changing list of your Solid songs, favoring ones you haven't sung in a while. It's built for picking your next song live at karaoke, one-handed.

Songbook is everything: search, filter by status, sort by fit or note. That's where you add, edit, and manage songs.
Song statuses
Solid — ready to sing tonight. Feeds Sing Now, your auto vocal range, and Recommendations.
Learning — actively working on it.
Maybe — an uncertain candidate, not committed yet.
Suggested — came from Recommendations, not yet reviewed.
Retired — used to sing it, not anymore. Kept for history.
Test — want to try it live before committing to Solid or Learning.
Vocal range
Set in Settings, two ways: Auto calculates your range from your Solid songs' known low/high notes — it needs a few Solid songs with ranges filled in before it can compute anything. Manual lets you type your own low/high notes directly.

Once a range is set, every song shows a colored bar: green means it's within your range, red means it isn't. Until you set one, songs show "your range not set" instead of a guess.
The "K" badge
A gold "K" next to a title means that song is confirmed available in the KaraFun catalog — so you know it's actually playable at a KaraFun-powered karaoke night, not just something you'd like to sing.
Recommendations
Tap the Recommendations icon (top right) for new song ideas, pulled three ways: more from artists you're already Solid on, other artists sharing a genre tag with your Solid songs, and — if configured — artists genuinely similar to your Solid artists via Last.fm. Every suggestion is checked against your vocal range before it's shown; anything without a researched range yet is left out and flagged separately.
Setlists
Tap the + button on the Setlists tab to build a named setlist for a specific gig — pick songs from your Songbook, reorder them, and have a plan ready before you walk in.
Logging performances
Tap "Sing it" on Sing Now, or log manually from a song's card in the Songbook. Performance history is what powers "last played" and keeps Sing Now rotating instead of repeating your favorites every night.
